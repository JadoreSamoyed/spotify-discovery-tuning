# Refine the listening brief

A short instruction such as “more bass-driven songs, fewer slow tracks” can tune the next refresh. Update only what the listener addressed. Keep hard exclusions separate from exploratory preferences.

| Listener says | Apply to future picks |
| --- | --- |
| “More like this song, including other artists.” | Retain what the listener says they liked and widen the artist pool. |
| “I like the artist, but this song is overplayed.” | Set that song aside. Keep the artist eligible. Leave fatigue duration unspecified unless the listener gives one. |
| “Too mellow for walking.” | Adjust the playlist context. |
| “Don't add this artist again.” | Carry the explicit artist exclusion into the requested playlist's future updates. |
| “I skipped the third one.” | Ask for the title if order is ambiguous. A skip gives no reliable reason or preference by itself. |

Keep user statements, your inferences, and proposed changes distinct in handoffs or private scheduled state. Never merge preferences across listeners. A scheduled brief revision guides future selections. Removing tracks already in the playlist is a separate action.
